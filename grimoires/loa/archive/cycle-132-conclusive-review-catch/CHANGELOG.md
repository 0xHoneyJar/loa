## [2.0.0-rc.7] — 2026-10-24 — The conclusive review-catch answer (release candidate)

> The rc.7 hold declared in Sprint 269 is lifted: the cycle-132 reads and the Phase 3 proof landed (REGISTRATION-15 for the reads, REGISTRATION-16/17 for the reproduction tooling; `grimoires/loa/reports/review-catch-c132/report.md`). The answer, in one sentence: the pipeline caught about ten points more of the labelled defects than a same-dollar bundle of bare Opus 5.5 sessions, inside an interval from −0.09 to +0.29 that includes zero and the MDE, with the stopping rule not firing and a quarter of the full arm lost to a structural limitation — not proven at this corpus size, and nothing shipped by default changes.

### Measured (cycle-132 Sprints 270–271 — the conclusive review-catch answer, 2026-10-21 → 2026-10-23)

- **Phase 1 as registered** (Sprint 270, FR-8; REGISTRATION-1 … REGISTRATION-14 at pipeline pin `fbd36a0c`). Fourteen unseen record fixtures (pr-11, 14, 15, 16, 19 … 28), five arms (`a1_full`, `a0_bare_matched`, `a0_bare_fable_matched`, `a1_review_only`, `a1_full_openmount`), two trials each, three PR groups with a registered look after each: 140 rows (`grimoires/loa/reports/review-catch-c132/rows.jsonl`), every invalid unit hand-read (`batch-scans.md`), the blind judge on 1,903 stratified items (`judge/result.md`: κ 0.20 strict, 0.27 lenient against grader 1.3.8).
- **The reads** (Sprint 271, FR-9; REGISTRATION-15 = analysis addendum 16; DEC-0801 … DEC-0814). Primary Δ recall(`a1_full`) − recall(`a0_bare_matched`) = **+0.097**, 95 % cluster-t **[−0.094, 0.288]** on 10 valid PRs, 16 pairs, 175 defects; REML +0.061 [−0.054, 0.177]; PR-level permutation p 0.37; the stopping rule did not fire and the final look reads **INCONCLUSIVE**; the full arm lost 11 of 28 planned cells (oracle exposure 8, contamination 5, refusal 1), so the 25 % guard trips and the read is inconclusive by degradation. Secondaries: defects per USD 0.41 vs 0.29 and the matched bundle **dominated** (17 fewer defects for USD 53 / 85 more); review-only − full −0.119 [−0.196, −0.041]; matched-Fable +0.333 [0.212, 0.455]; pipeline-independent +0.294 [−0.044, 0.632] on 7 PRs. Positive control **PASS** (+0.042, 9 exposed open runs); placebo **FAIL** on calibration (0.100 vs 0.08; bias passes) with the registered §8 fallback applied. Sensitivities in the registered order: the frozen c131 and v1 keys read `too_few_prs` (3 PRs), the seeded review-derived key reverses the sign (−0.088 [−0.303, 0.128], 9 PRs, 57 defects), re-including oracle-exposed cells lifts the estimate off zero (+0.166 [0.032, 0.299]). Exploratory, post hoc (DEC-0800, DEC-0813): about a quarter to a third of grader-credited cell–defect pairs carry a citation a blind judge says describes the defect, in every closed-mount arm alike.
- **The report** (Sprint 271, FR-9). `grimoires/loa/reports/review-catch-c132/report.md` (ELI5 ≤ 300 words; the answer; reads; stopping rule; sensitivities; placebo; positive control; ground truth; judge; spend; caveats; proof; files) is assembled from the artefacts by `scripts/report-assemble.py` — every table row byte for byte from `reads.md`, the placebos, the judge results, the spend meter, the hand-reads, the annotation result — and `evals/tests/c132-report.bats` fails on any row that appears in none of them. - **Analysis addendum 16** (Sprint 271; DEC-0809, DEC-0814). `evals/analysis/c132/regrade_labels.py` re-grades hard-link copies of every cell under the frozen c131, v1 and review-derived keys (alternate suite roots re-frozen with `freeze.sh`; sessions then `--union`; a seeded `--check`), never touching a recorded run directory; `reads.py` gains `review_derived_labels` and the §5 co-reads in `--final`; `stats.py` prints `dominated` / `dominated_by_cost` where the registration says so; `judge_defects.py` samples and aggregates the exploratory defect-level judge read.

### Fixed

- **The closed mount refuses an unavailable fix commit** (PR #1286 Bridgebuilder F001, HIGH). `lib/sandbox-mount.sh overlay_answer_paths` exits 2 before writing anything when `fixture.yaml`'s `fix_commit` is not in the source tree: Sprint 263 logged `fix_commit_unavailable` and overlaid the PR paths only, which left the fix-only paths at the later copy while `check_mount_closed` called the mount closed. `check_mount_closed` and `took-effect.sh` rule `oracle_closed` now fail on `overlay_fix_commit=unavailable` as well. No recorded run was affected: `overlay_fix_commit=available` on all 18 closed-mount cycle-131 runs, `none` on the 4 whose fixture declares no fix commit.
- **Grader 1.3.1: the oracle detector scans error-marked tool results** (PR #1286 Bridgebuilder F002, MEDIUM). A differing `diff` exits 1 and Claude Code marks the result `is_error` with the output after an `Exit code 1` line; 1.3.0 skipped every such result. 1.3.1 strips the line and scans the rest: the diff form needs printed ranges near a planted site (no must_match-only credit), the read form later-only text, and an error-marked section without ranges is never `oracle_exposed_unknown` (a refusal or a hook block names the paths and prints no diff). Recall and contamination are unchanged. The 36 cycle-130 and 27 cycle-131 review-catch runs re-graded under 1.3.1 are identical on every oracle field, recall and contamination (63 of 63); the 26 error-marked results carrying diff ranges in those transcripts all compare `pr/base` with `pr/head` or read `head.diff`, never the mount copy. The pinned 1.3.0 rows remain the main read (DEC-0648); the 1.3.1 grades are a labelled sensitivity.
