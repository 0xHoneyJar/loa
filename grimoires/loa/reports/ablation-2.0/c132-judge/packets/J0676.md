## Finding

``` text
- **MEDIUM** (confidence: high) `head/.claude/adapters/cheval.py:1843` — `_ceiling_unverified` is assigned once from the head entry's pre-flight outcome and never recomputed in the chain loop, yet it selects the error class at `head/.claude/adapters/cheval.py:2256` (`CEILING_UNVERIFIED_LIMIT` vs `PROVIDER_CONTEXT_LIMIT`) and the non-walk short-circuit at `head/.claude/adapters/cheval.py:2297` for every fallback hop. Scenario: opt-in, head `claude-opus-5` dispatches 600K above its probed bound (unverified=True), fails with a walkable 503; the fallback is a calibrated entry (900K); its ordinary 429 now exits `RATE_LIMITED` with `calibration_needed` written for the fallback and a `RATE_LIMIT_UNVERIFIED` row appended for an entry that was never unverified. The converse (verified head, unverified fallback) mis-classes a provider verdict as `PROVIDER_CONTEXT_LIMIT`.
**Suggestion:** Compute the status per hop from the `input_bound` decision already made in `_lookup_max_input_tokens` (return the `CeilingDecision`, or recompute via `_ceiling_input_bound(_hop_entry, max_tokens=_hop_budget, observed=...)`): `unverified = policy == "derived" and not decision.calibrated and estimate > decision.probed`. Keep the head's flag for the envelope only.
```
