head/.claude/skills/bridgebuilder-review/resources/config.ts:684 — The head-only check only asks whether `keyless` / `insecure` are set, not what they point at. That breaks the D-7.5 claim that provider declarations come from trusted config only. Compare the whole declaration (endpoint, opt-in host, auth) with the trusted one, or refuse any head declaration that differs from it.

Bridgebuilder and cheval now disagree on which providers are keyless: Bridgebuilder marks a literal-key provider valid with no credential check, while cheval sends that literal as a Bearer token. Restrict this to `"local"` (case-insensitive, trimmed) to match D-8.0.
