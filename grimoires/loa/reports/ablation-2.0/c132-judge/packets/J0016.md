head/.claude/scripts/lib/dec-write-lib.sh:513 — `op_confirm` runs `derive_attribution 0`, which uses `is_unattended`. That check includes the PRD `**Status:**` signal (`prd prd-status:`), and line 509 deliberately lets that signal through confirm ("the PRD Status line alone does not block it"). When the PRD Status mentions autonomous/unattended, the operator's `--answer-ref cli:<now>` is dropped with a WARN, so line 538 records `decided_by: agent` (the proposing voice) for a row the operator confirmed in person. The row then shows in every "Decisions taken on your behalf" render as agent-decided. Only the live signals should drop the ref, or confirm should not call `is_unattended` at all.
