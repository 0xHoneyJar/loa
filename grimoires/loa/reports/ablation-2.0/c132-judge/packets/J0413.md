head/evals/harness/lib/seed_tasks.py:601 — When a selected candidate fails its proof, or is refused by the secret scan (line 591), `taken.discard(tid)` hands its id to the next candidate, but the half-built `<stage>/<tid>/` directory is never removed. Reproduced: a "fix:" commit that is green at its parent, followed by a clean fix, gives `written 0, proof failed 2`, exit 1. Fix: remove the stage task dir before discarding the id, or build each task in its own mkdtemp.

The seeder silently writes fewer than N tasks even when enough candidates qualify.
