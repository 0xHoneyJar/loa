Each: change X because measurement Y, testable by Z (the A/B that would prove it wrong). None applied in this task; each becomes a `cycle-131-candidate` bead.

Cost of the A/B ≈ USD 35. Catch-per-dollar: this is a measurement fix — it changes what every later number means, so it ranks first.
2. **Grade with multi-site labels and a same-function rule (or require range citations from every arm)** because bare described d1/d3/d7 of pr-12 and d5 of pr-13 at the bug line while the anchors sit on the function header or a fix-region line, and re-grading at "±3 or same function" halves the gap (+0.263 → +0.140) — testable by re-grading the existing 24 runs under the new rule (USD 0) and by a 20-sample hand adjudication of the credits it adds (two labelers, kappa reported). Another measurement fix; second.
3. **Skip the AUDIT gate on review-only PRs (run it only when the review verdict is APPROVED, or on diffs above N lines)** because the audit gate is 49 % of a full run (USD 5.70) and removing its citations costs 0.055 of task-mean catch (0.854 → 0.799; two defect-trials of 46 were audit-first) — testable by an `a1_review_only` arm on the four PRs × 3 trials (≈ USD 70): prove it wrong if the catch drops by more than 0.10 or the verdict accuracy falls.
4. **Make the dissent cite lines or drop it from review-catch gates** because no envelope carries a numeric anchor, the dissent is credited with 0 citations in 24 gates, its causal contribution on pr-12 is ≈ 2 defect-trials of 24, and it costs 17 % of a full run plus the 420 s cap kills and the 200 c budget refusals (pr-13 never had a voice) — testable by `a3_one_voice` / no-dissent arms on pr-12 × 3 (≈ USD 45) and by counting numeric anchors in envelopes after an anchor-format change: prove it wrong if the no-dissent arm loses more than 0.08 catch on pr-12.
5. **Route by diff size: two bare passes (USD 1.4) for PRs under ~200 lines, the full gate only above** because bare saturates on the two small PRs (9/9, 8/9), two bare passes reach 0.744 task-mean at 12 % of full's cost, and the full pipeline's measurable lift lives on the 1,077-line PR — testable by adding two more small corpus PRs (pr-14, pr-15 or synthetic) under both arms (≈ USD 30): prove it wrong if full beats bare ×2 on a small PR by more than 0.10.
