- cost-of-remembering paper source (paper/sections/*.tex, fetched 2026-10-14; LaTeX quoted as returned). Abstract: "A memory made of files answers about as well as putting the entire history in a frontier model's context window ... while reading 97% fewer tokens per question and costing 95% less." and "Our headline result is that two approaches are hard to tell apart, not that one wins." Results: file-based memory reads "a catalog and two or three files, 5314 tokens" against the baseline's "184186" (every conversation), "97.1% fewer tokens and 95.2% less money per question"; build cost "$2.46" per memory, "$0.0517" per filed session (48 conversations), "$0.0177" per query; break-even "about seven questions" (every history crosses between 5.7 and 8.7, median 6.9); three arms: long context, guided ICM agent, unguided agent; "The unguided agent is significantly worse than long context (p = 0.021)", "The ICM agent is not (p = 0.227)", "The two file-based arms do not separate from each other (p = 0.125, six discordant questions to one)"; in absolute terms "Long context answered more questions correctly than ICM ... 34 of 39 against 29 of 39, or 0.872 against 0.744"; marginal filing cost grows ("conversation eight cost $0.0781 against $0.0655 for conversation one"); portability: memory filed by "Claude Sonnet 5 and queried by GPT-5.5" "scored identically to that model reading the full history, at 96% lower cost per query". Limitations (05-limitations.tex): "Only one accuracy comparison in this paper reaches significance"; about 90 questions needed for power versus 39 run; "the accuracy ordering reversed twice between five questions and thirty-nine"; the task "approaches ceiling" at baseline 0.872 so accuracy cannot discriminate; prices are "list prices on a single date", "the ratios are the durable part; the absolute dollars are not"; the benchmark cannot assess collaboration, auditability, correction, month-long horizons or hybrid storage. paper/README.md status: "Written against the n=20 results. The n=40 runs are the ones that determine whether the guided-vs-autonomous comparison can be stated at all"; rule "Do not type a number into a .tex file" (tables generated from run summaries). Treat as a small, self-run, single-domain study: the cost ratio is the citable part; the accuracy parity is not established (29/39 vs 34/39, underpowered).
