## 1. Executive summary

1. **The framework's gates do not govern the framework's own `main`.** 12 of the last 15 merged PRs were self-merged by one admin with zero approving reviews, including #1251 (192 files, +10,697/−3,708, hooks + adapters + release pipeline). Branch protection has `enforce_admins=false` and `require_code_owner_reviews=false`. Fix the protection settings first; everything else is downstream.
2. **The one-way verdict rule is enforced by `verdict-derive.sh` and then ignored by its consumer.** An APPROVED trailer with `critical=1` makes `golden-path.sh` report the sprint as reviewed (live test). `/run` still detects review outcome by hashing prose. One small `/bug` closes the honesty gap the cycle-119 "mechanical floor" claims.
3. **Headless model identity is wrong in the ledgers today.** A live `claude-headless` call returned Fable content, was billed as `claude-haiku-4-5` at $0.50 for 13 output tokens, and was audited as `claude-headless`. RFC #1006 has described this since June; nothing is implemented. Gemini headless is dead on any host without `agy`.
4. **State has no lifecycle.** A `RUNNING` run-state has blocked the Stop hook every turn for 49 days; 54 of 84 ledger cycles are "active"; cycles 117–120 and 123 were never registered; 10 `mktemp` leftovers are tracked in git under `.claude/`; tests write into the production cost and MODELINV ledgers.
5. **Nothing has shipped since Sept 14, the latest tag cannot complete a mount, and the backlog is ~55% already fixed.** The post-merge pipeline now parks an unapproved release candidate; tags, GitHub Releases and CHANGELOG disagree; a fresh mount of v1.202.1 aborts at the Aleph step that main already fixed; `check-loa.sh` reports 173 integrity failures on a pristine main. 30 of 55 open issues are fixed on main by #1251 commits, and the local cycle-123 branch duplicated all 15 of its fixes.
