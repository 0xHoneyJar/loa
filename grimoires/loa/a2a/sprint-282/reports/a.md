6. busybox and mawk disagree on a NUL inside a KF line (pre-existing; interpreter input handling: mawk deletes the NUL and keeps the rest, busybox ends the line at the NUL; both keep NUL and control bytes out of the output). The portability test asserts byte-identity on a NUL-free fixture (shapes, a 3-byte UTF-8 symptom over 100 bytes, `\001`, `\177`); a separate test pins the NUL-safety invariant.
