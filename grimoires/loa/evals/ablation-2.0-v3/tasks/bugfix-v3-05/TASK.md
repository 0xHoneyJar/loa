# Task: the sensitivity family re-includes oracle-exposed cells by the form of the exposure, and a cell records that form

`evals/analysis/c132/reads.py` reads the cycle-132 ablation. The primary read excludes every invalid cell; the registered sensitivity family `contaminated_reincluded_per_class` (`reincluded_family(cells, reg, seed)`) re-reads the primary with cells whose ONLY invalidity is a contamination of one class counted valid — one member per observed class under `classes`, plus `all_classes` — and counts them per arm and group. A full-pipeline cell can also be invalid because its session was exposed to the oracle (`oracle_exposed_defects` on the row and the grade); the grade's `oracle_exposed_by_form` says how: `{"diff": [...], "read": [...]}` (defects seen through a diff, defects seen through a read), or is absent.

**Bug (observed):** an oracle-exposed cell is excluded and never re-read in any sensitivity; the family carries no member for it, `reads.json` and `reads.md` say nothing about how many cells were exposed in which form, and a cell records nothing about the form of its exposure beside its validity reason `oracle_exposed`. `common` has no notion of an exposure form.

**Expected:**
- `common.oracle_form(by_form)` returns `read` when the record lists no diff defect and at least one read defect, `diff` when it lists a diff defect, and `unknown` for an absent record. A cell records its form (`oracle_form`) beside its validity reasons; the reason itself stays `oracle_exposed`, as the existing checks expect.
- The family gains `oracle_exposed`: `{"status": "no_oracle_exposed_cells"}` when no full-pipeline cell is oracle-exposed; otherwise `status` `ok`, three reads — `read_only` (the exposed cells whose form is `read` counted valid), `diff` (the cells whose form is `diff`), `all` (every oracle-exposed cell, whatever its form, `unknown` included) — each with `reincluded_keys` (sorted `<batch>/<arm>/<task>/<trial>`), `n_pairs` and `recall_full`, and `counts` as `{arm: {group: {task: {form: n}}}}`. A cell that is oracle-exposed AND contaminated (or otherwise invalid) is never re-included, in no member. The primary filter and the contamination members are unchanged: a contamination member never picks up an oracle-exposed cell.
- `reads.md` describes the new reads (`re-included: oracle-exposed, read form only`, and so on) and lists the counts as `<arm> / <group> / <task>: <form> <n>`.

Fix the bug in place and add a regression test of your own. Run the tests under `evals/analysis/c132/tests` that cover the code you change, with `python3 -m pytest -q <file>`.
