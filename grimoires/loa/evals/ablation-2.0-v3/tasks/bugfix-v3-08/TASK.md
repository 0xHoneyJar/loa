# Task: the decision table gains NEGATIVE — a significantly negative Δ̂ is its own outcome, SMALL and YES need Δ̂ ≥ 0, and the power study tallies it

`evals/analysis/c132/reads.py` decides each look of the cycle-132 ablation with `decide(...)` from the standardised lift `z_yes = Δ̂ / SE` against the boundary `b_yes`, the futility statistic `z_no = (MDE − Δ̂) / SE` against `b_no`, the upper end of the reduced interval `rci_hi`, the two conditional powers `cp_yes`, `cp_no`, and whether the look is final; `OUTCOMES` lists the outcomes and `PARTITION_LEGEND` explains them in `reads.md`. `evals/analysis/c132/power.py` simulates looks and `evaluate(...)` tallies the share of each outcome; `summarise(...)` builds per-N rows and `render_md` the table.

**Bug (observed):** a Δ̂ far below zero — the full pipeline clearly worse than the matched bare arm — is read as `NO`, the same outcome as a Δ̂ of zero; `SMALL` and `YES` can be reached by a negative Δ̂ (`|z_yes|` crosses the boundary whatever the sign); the power study has no column for a negative finding, so its shares cannot show how often a harmful effect would be recognised.

**Expected:**
- `decide(delta, z_yes, b_yes, z_no, b_no, rci_hi, mde, cp_yes, cp_no, final)` — `delta` is the new first argument — is the ordered, exhaustive table: 1. `NEGATIVE` when `|z_yes| ≥ b_yes` and Δ̂ < 0 (this wins even when `z_no` also crosses); 2. `YES` when `z_yes ≥ b_yes`, `z_no < b_no` and `rci_hi ≥ mde` (exactly at the MDE is YES); 3. `SMALL` when `z_yes ≥ b_yes` and either `z_no ≥ b_no` or `rci_hi < mde`; 4. `NO` when `z_no ≥ b_no`; 5. `INCONCLUSIVE` on a final look, else 6. `FUTILE` when both conditional powers are below 0.20, else `CONTINUE`. Rules 2 and 3 only ever fire with Δ̂ ≥ 0; a negative Δ̂ that does not cross the boundary is `NO` when `z_no` crosses. `OUTCOMES` includes `NEGATIVE`; `PARTITION_LEGEND` explains it.
- `power.evaluate(...)` returns a `negative` share beside `yes`, `no`, `small`, `futile`, `inconclusive` and `void`; the shares other than `void` sum to 1 (two of four simulated studies ending in a significantly negative Δ̂ give `negative` 0.5). The per-N rows carry `negative_at_null` and `negative_at_mde` (and `null` / `mde` carry `negative`), and the rendered table has a `| NEGATIVE |` column.
